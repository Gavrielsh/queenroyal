## Our commitment

QueenRoyal is meant to be entertainment. We want play to stay fun, and we support players who
want to set boundaries around their play.

## Tools to manage your play

Self-exclusion, cool-off periods, and deposit, loss, and session limits are on our roadmap
(docs/PLAN.md item 6) and are not yet available in the app. This page will be updated with
instructions once those controls ship.

## Warning signs

Consider taking a break if you notice yourself: playing longer than you intend to, spending
money you had planned to use elsewhere, chasing losses, or feeling anxious or irritable when
you try to stop.

## Getting help

If you or someone you know may have a gambling problem, free and confidential help is
available:

- **National Council on Problem Gambling:** 1-800-522-4700, available 24/7
- **ncpgambling.org**

## Age restriction

QueenRoyal is restricted to players 18 years of age or older.
