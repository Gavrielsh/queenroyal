## 1. Information we collect

Account information you provide (email, date of birth, state of residence), identity
verification documents when you complete KYC, and gameplay and device information needed to
operate and secure the service.

## 2. How we use it

To create and secure your account, verify eligibility (age and state of residence), process
prize redemptions, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with sweepstakes and anti-money
laundering obligations.

## 3. Sharing

We share information with identity-verification providers, payment processors, and regulators
or law enforcement where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Cookies

We use cookies necessary to keep you signed in and to remember your session. See your browser
settings to manage cookies.

## 5. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or
storage is completely secure.

## 6. Retention

We retain account and transaction records for as long as required by applicable sweepstakes,
tax, and anti-money-laundering regulations.

## 7. Your rights

You may request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of your personal information, subject to
our record-keeping obligations, by contacting support.

## 8. Children's privacy

QueenRoyal is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from
anyone under 18.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the version and effective date above reflect the current policy.
