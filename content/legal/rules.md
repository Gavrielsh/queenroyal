## NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN

A purchase will not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited by law.

## 1. Sponsor

QueenRoyal (the "Sponsor"). Contact details are listed in the app.

## 2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States (excluding states listed as excluded at the time
of entry) who are 18 years of age or older. Employees of the Sponsor and their immediate
family members are not eligible. Void where prohibited.

## 3. How to enter

There are two free ways to obtain Sweeps Coins (SC), and no purchase is required for either:

- **Play.** SC may be awarded through free daily bonuses and in-game promotions.
- **Alternative Method of Entry (AMOE).** A free entry route is available at `/free-entry`
  for players who prefer not to play, following the instructions on that page.

Gold Coins (GC) may optionally be purchased for entertainment play; GC purchases never grant
additional SC and are never required to enter or to win.

## 4. Sweeps Coins and prizes

SC obtained through play or AMOE start as SC_UNPLAYED and must be played through the current
playthrough requirement before becoming SC_REDEEMABLE. Only SC_REDEEMABLE winnings are
eligible for cash prize redemption, subject to identity verification (KYC), minimum redemption
amounts, and daily or account redemption caps described at redemption time.

## 5. SC expiry

Unplayed or redeemable Sweeps Coins may expire after a period of account inactivity. The
current expiry window is shown on your account balance screen.

## 6. Odds

Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received and the game played.

## 7. General conditions

The Sponsor reserves the right to disqualify any entrant found to be tampering with the entry
process, violating these rules, or acting in an unsportsmanlike or disruptive manner. The
Sponsor's decisions are final in all matters relating to this sweepstakes.

## 8. Amendment

These rules may be updated; the version and effective date at the top of this page reflect the
current terms.
